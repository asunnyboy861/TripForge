## Description
Plan your perfect trip in seconds with AI. TripForge is the smart travel planner that builds personalized day-by-day itineraries, organizes your bookings, and keeps everything in one place.

AI-POWERED TRIP PLANNING
Just tell TripForge where you are going and when - our AI creates a complete day-by-day itinerary with activities, restaurants, and attractions tailored to your interests. No more hours of research across dozens of tabs.

ALL YOUR BOOKINGS, ORGANIZED
Forward your confirmation emails and TripForge automatically extracts flight details, hotel reservations, and activity bookings. Everything is organized chronologically in one clean timeline.

INTERACTIVE MAP VIEW
See your entire trip on a beautiful map. Drag and drop activities to optimize your route, check walking distances between stops, and never waste time backtracking across town.

DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY
Your trip is organized into clear daily schedules with times, locations, and notes. Add custom activities, reorder plans, and add personal notes to make each day perfect.

BOOKING MANAGEMENT
Keep all your travel confirmations in one place. TripForge parses email confirmations for flights, hotels, car rentals, and activities - no more digging through your inbox at check-in.

ICLOUD SYNC
Your trips sync seamlessly across all your Apple devices via iCloud. Plan on your iPad, reference on your iPhone while traveling. Always up to date, always accessible.

DESIGNED FOR APPLE
Built with SwiftUI for a native iOS experience. Supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, and integrates with Maps, Calendar, and your photo library. Feels right at home on your iPhone and iPad.
